# <i class='fas fa-cube'></i>&nbsp;3D

Learn Godot 4 3D game development with recipes for Node3D, CharacterBody3D, Camera3D, ray casts, player movement, and vehicle controls. Begin with [CharacterBody3D Movement](/godot_recipes/4.x/3d/characterbody3d_examples/) and [Camera Gimbal](/godot_recipes/4.x/3d/camera_gimbal/), then explore [Click to Move](/godot_recipes/4.x/3d/click_to_move/) and [3D Shooting](/godot_recipes/4.x/3d/3d_shooting/) to build a complete control scheme.
